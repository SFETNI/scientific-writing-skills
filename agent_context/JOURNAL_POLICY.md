## Common elements across all publishers

All major publishers share these principles as of 2026:

1. **Author accountability**: AI assistance does not reduce the author's responsibility for accuracy, integrity, and originality.
2. **Mandatory disclosure**: Use of AI writing tools must be declared — typically in Methods, Acknowledgements, or a dedicated AI disclosure statement.
3. **No AI authorship**: AI tools cannot be listed as authors under any publisher's policy.
4. **No AI-generated images for data figures**: Figures representing research data must reflect actual measurements or analyses, not AI-generated content.
5. **Human review of all AI output**: Authors are expected to review, validate, and take responsibility for all AI-assisted text.

## Disclosure template (adapt to your journal)

Fill in the AI disclosure statement before submission:

```
AI USE DISCLOSURE
This manuscript was prepared with the assistance of [AI tool name(s)]. These tools were used for
[specific purpose: language editing / structure review / citation checking / ...].
All scientific content, claims, data interpretations, and conclusions are the responsibility of
the authors. The authors have reviewed all AI-assisted content and take full responsibility for
the accuracy and integrity of this work.
```

## Important note

This file provides a starting-point reference. Always consult the official author guidelines for your specific target journal before submission, as:
- Policies vary between journals within the same publisher family.
- Policies are updated regularly (several major publishers updated their AI policies in 2024–2025).
- Some journals have stricter requirements than the publisher-level defaults.
